# Living Work

Living Work is a small homeschooling experiment from Parents’ Nook Lab. It helps parents plan with books they trust, teach at a pace that fits their family, and keep a private record of what they want to revisit.

A parent starts with a learning goal, available time, and selected book references. A planning assistant can turn the brief the parent reviews into a draft. The parent can revise it and choose what to teach. Over time, the choices they make about lessons, plans, and notes can shape how they use the tool.
